Assamese Fish Curry (Masor Tenga)

Light, tangy fish curry — tomato, lemon, tamarind or khaar can be used as the souring agent.

⭐ Rating4.4 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields3–4 servings
TimePrep 10–15 min • Cook 15–20 min

Ingredients

Main

  • 600 g river fish (rohu, seer, or similar), cut into steaks
  • 2–3 medium tomatoes (or 2 tbsp lemon juice)
  • 1 tsp turmeric, salt to taste

For cooking

  • 2–3 tbsp mustard oil (or neutral oil)
  • 2–3 green chilies, coriander for garnish
  • 1 cup water (adjust for gravy consistency)
Tossing taste!

Method

Marinate & Fry

Marinate: Dust fish with turmeric and salt; rest 10 minutes.

Fry: Heat mustard oil until it reaches smoking point then cool slightly; shallow fry fish until golden. Remove and set aside.

Tangy sauce & finish

Sauce: In the same oil, sauté chopped tomatoes with a pinch of salt until soft and pulpy. Add 1 cup water and bring to a simmer. (Alternatively use tamarind, lemon or khaar/elephant apple.)

Finish: Gently add fried fish into simmering sauce and cook 6–8 minutes on low — avoid vigorous stirring to keep fish intact. Add slit green chilies and coriander. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Watch this recipe

Tips & Serving

For authentic flavor try khaar or elephant apple if available. Adjust sourness (tomato/tamarind/lemon) to taste. Be gentle when stirring to keep fish steaks intact.
