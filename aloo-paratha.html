Aloo Paratha

Stuffed whole-wheat parathas with spiced potato filling — breakfast or comfort lunch.

⭐ Rating4.7 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields6–8 parathas
TimePrep 20 min • Cook 20–25 min

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta) + water + pinch of salt
  • Ghee or oil for cooking

Stuffing

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped; 2 tbsp coriander
  • 1 tsp cumin powder; 1 tsp chaat masala or amchur; salt to taste

Method

Prepare dough

1. Dough: Mix atta and salt, add water gradually, knead to a soft pliable dough. Rest 15–20 minutes.

Make stuffing

2. Stuffing: Mash potatoes well. Mix with chilies, coriander, cumin, amchur, salt. Keep relatively dry — add roasted semolina if too moist.

Assemble & cook

3. Assemble: Roll small dough disc, add stuffing, seal edges, flatten and roll into 6–7" paratha.

4. Cook: Cook on hot tawa with ghee/oil until golden and slightly crisp. Serve hot with yogurt and pickle.

Watch this recipe

Tips & Serving

Keep stuffing dry to ease rolling; press gently while cooking to get even puffing. Serve hot with butter, yogurt or mango pickle.
