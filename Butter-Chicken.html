Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Iconic creamy tomato-cashew gravy with tender marinated chicken — slightly smoky, buttery and luxurious.

⭐ Rating4.8 ★
DifficultyMedium
Serves4
TimePrep 20–30 min (+marination) • Cook 25–30 min

Ingredients

For the Marinade

  • 700 g boneless chicken (thighs preferred), cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup yogurt (thick curd)
  • 1 tbsp ginger–garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil

For the Gravy

  • 3–4 large tomatoes (or 1½ cups tomato puree)
  • ¼ cup cashews (soaked 15 min)
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (optional)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp ginger–garlic paste
  • Salt to taste

Method

1. Marinate the chicken

Mix the marinade: In a large bowl combine yogurt, ginger–garlic paste, red chili powder, garam masala, lemon juice, oil and salt. Whisk well so spices distribute evenly.

Add chicken: Toss the chicken pieces into the marinade until fully coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2–4 hours; overnight is best for maximum tenderness and flavor.

2. Cook the chicken

Grill: Brush lightly with oil and grill until edges char slightly.

Pan-sear: Heat a pan and sear until browned and cooked through.

Bake: Roast at 200°C (400°F) until edges brown. Slight charring is desirable. Set cooked chicken aside.

3. Make the tomato–cashew puree

Sauté: Heat butter or oil, cook chopped tomatoes with ginger–garlic until soft and oil separates.

Add cashews: Stir in soaked cashews and simmer briefly. Blend to a smooth puree for a silky gravy.

4. Prepare the main gravy & finish

Melt butter: Heat 4 tbsp butter, add puree and cook 6–8 minutes until oil separates.

Add cream: Lower heat and add ½ cup cream; don't boil vigorously afterward.

Combine: Add chicken, simmer 6–8 minutes. Finish with crushed kasuri methi, a swirl of cream and a cube of butter.

Notes & Serving Suggestions

Garnish with a swirl of cream and a small cube of butter. Serve hot with naan, lachha paratha, or jeera rice. For extra smokiness, lightly char the tomatoes or grill the marinated chicken before adding to the gravy.

Store leftovers refrigerated (2–3 days). Reheat gently and add a splash of water or cream if it thickens.
