Momos

Steamed Himalayan dumplings — vegetable or chicken filling, served with spicy chutney.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields~30 momos

Ingredients

Dough

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour, pinch salt, water to knead

Fillings

  • Veg: 3 cups finely shredded cabbage + carrot + onions, 2–3 cloves garlic, 1" ginger, soy sauce, salt, pepper
  • Chicken: 500 g minced chicken, finely chopped onions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, pepper, salt

Method

Make dough

Dough: Mix flour and salt; add water gradually and knead to a firm, smooth dough. Rest 20–30 minutes.

Prepare filling

Filling: Mix finely chopped veg or minced chicken with seasonings. Keep mixture slightly moist but not watery.

Shape & steam

Shape: Roll small discs (≈3"). Place 1 tsp filling in centre, pleat and seal edges neatly.

Steam: Arrange on steamer lined with cabbage leaf or oiled parchment and steam 10–12 minutes until translucent (veg) or cooked (meat). For crisp momos, shallow fry after steaming (optional).

Watch this recipe

Tips

Don’t overstuff; ensure edges are thin to allow neat pleats. Keep filling chilled while shaping. Serve with spicy tomato-garlic chutney. For best results rest dough and work with small batches.
