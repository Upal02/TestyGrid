Palak Paneer

Spinach puree curry with paneer cubes — nutritious, creamy and quick.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields4 servings
TimePrep 10–15 min • Cook 15–20 min

Ingredients

Main

  • 300–350 g fresh spinach, washed & trimmed
  • 200–250 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes (or ½ cup tomato puree)

Pantry

  • 1 tbsp ginger–garlic paste
  • 1–2 green chilies (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 2–3 tbsp cream (optional), ½ tsp garam masala, salt to taste

Method

Blanch & puree spinach

1. Blanch: Boil water, add spinach for 1–2 minutes until wilted. Immediately dunk in ice water to keep bright green color.

2. Purée: Drain and blend spinach to a smooth puree, adding a little water if needed.

Prepare paneer & masala

Paneer: Optionally shallow-fry cubes until golden at edges; set aside.

Masala: Heat oil/ghee, sauté onions till translucent. Add ginger–garlic and green chilies; cook till fragrant. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.

Combine & finish

Combine: Add spinach puree to masala and simmer 5–8 minutes. Add paneer cubes and simmer 3–4 minutes.

Finish: Stir in cream if using and garam masala. Adjust salt and serve hot with roti or naan.

Tips

For silkier texture add cashew paste. Avoid overcooking puree to retain brightness.

Notes & Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with roti, naan or jeera rice. Add a spoon of cashew paste for extra silkiness. Avoid overcooking the spinach puree to keep the color vibrant.
