Vada Pav

Mumbai’s ubiquitous “burger” — a spiced potato fritter sandwiched in a butter-toasted pav with chutneys.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields6 pavs • Prep: 25 min • Cook: 20 min

Ingredients

Vada

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds, a few curry leaves
  • 1 green chili finely chopped, 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Salt, fresh coriander to taste

Batter & Serve

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • ¼ tsp baking soda (optional), pinch of salt, ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Water to make a thick batter
  • 6 pav buns, butter for toasting; green chutney & dry garlic chutney

Method

Make spiced aloo

Make spiced aloo: Heat a little oil; temper mustard seeds and curry leaves. Add green chili and ginger then add mashed potato, season and mix. Shape into 6 balls once cool.

Batter & fry

Batter & fry: Mix besan, baking soda, salt and turmeric with water to a thick batter. Heat oil; dip potato balls in batter and deep fry until golden and crisp.

Assemble

Assemble: Slit pav, toast with butter on tawa, smear green chutney and dry garlic chutney, place vada inside and serve hot.

Tips & Variations

Crisp vada + warm pav = the perfect contrast. For a lighter version shallow-fry or air-fry the vadas. Add grated onion to the potato mix for extra texture. Store leftover vadas in the fridge and reheat in an oven to retain crunch.
