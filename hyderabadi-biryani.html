Hyderabadi Biryani

Layered dum-style biryani with saffron, fried onions, and marinated meat — aromatic and rich.

⭐ Rating4.6 ★
DifficultyHard
Yields4 servings
TimePrep 1–8 hrs (marinate) • Cook 60–75 min

Ingredients

Rice & meat

  • 500 g basmati rice (soaked 30 min)
  • 700 g marinated chicken or mutton (yogurt, ginger-garlic, red chili, biryani masala)

Flavours

  • 3 onions, fried till golden (birista)
  • Saffron soaked in milk, mint, coriander, ghee, whole spices

Method

Parboil rice

Cook rice: Boil salted water with whole spices. Cook rice ~70% done. Drain and spread to stop cooking.

Layering

Layer: In handi layer marinated meat with rice, sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk and ghee. Repeat layers.

Dum & serve

Dum: Seal pot with dough or lid. Cook on low heat 30–45 min (or place pot on tawa). Rest 10 minutes then fluff gently. Serve with raita and mirchi ka salan.

Watch this recipe

Tips

Use aged basmati for separate grains. Fry onions to deep golden for sweet caramel notes. Seal pot well for proper dum.
