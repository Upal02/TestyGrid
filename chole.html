Chole (Standalone)

Hearty chickpea curry — everyday comfort food. Serve with rice, roti or bhature.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields4 servings
TimePrep 15–20 min (+soak) • Cook 35–45 min

Ingredients

Chole

  • 1½ cups dried chickpeas (soak 8–10 hours)
  • 1 tea bag (optional — color)
  • 2 bay leaves, 1 black cardamom, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped; 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger–garlic paste; 1–2 green chilies
  • 1½ tbsp chole/chana masala; 1 tsp turmeric; 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala; salt; 3 tbsp oil or ghee; coriander & lemon

Serve with

  • Steamed rice, roti, poori or bhature
  • Sliced raw onions, lemon wedges, pickles
  • Optional: knob of ghee to finish

Method

1. Soak & cook chickpeas

Soak: Rinse and soak chickpeas 8–10 hours. Drain and place in pressure cooker with 3 cups water, pinch salt, bay leaf, black cardamom and optional tea bag. Cook ~6–7 whistles until tender but not mushy. Reserve cooking liquid.

2. Make the masala

Masala: Heat oil/ghee, add cumin seeds. Sauté onions till deep golden-brown. Add ginger–garlic, then tomato puree and spices. Cook till oil separates and masala darkens — patience here builds flavor.

3. Combine & simmer

Combine: Add cooked chickpeas with some cooking liquid. Bring to boil then simmer 20–25 minutes so flavors meld. Mash a few chickpeas against pot side to thicken naturally. Finish with garam masala and garnish.

Watch this recipe

Notes & Serving

For deeper color add a tea bag while cooking (remove later). If chickpeas are hard add a pinch of baking soda while cooking. Finish with ghee for richer flavor. Serve hot.
