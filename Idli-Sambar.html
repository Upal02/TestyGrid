Idli with Sambar

Steamed idlis served with tangy sambar and coconut chutney — a healthy breakfast classic.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Yields12–20 idlis (depends on mold)
TimePrep 6–12 hrs (ferment) • Steam 10–12 min

Ingredients

Idli batter

  • Idli/dosa batter (parboiled rice + regular rice + urad dal + fenugreek) — soak, grind & ferment

Sambar

  • 1 cup toor dal, assorted vegetables (brinjal, drumstick, carrot), tamarind pulp, sambar powder, curry leaves, mustard seeds, oil, salt

Method

Batter & Idli

Batter: Soak, grind and ferment batter as for dosa.

Steam idlis: Grease molds, fill ¾ and steam 10–12 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Cool briefly before unmolding.

Sambar

Cook dal: Pressure cook toor dal with turmeric until soft.

Vegetables: Boil vegetables with tamarind pulp, sambar powder and salt until tender. Add cooked dal and simmer.

Tempering: Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil and pour over sambar before serving.

Watch this recipe

Tips

Keep sambar slightly tangy; adjust tamarind. Let idli batter ferment in a warm place for airy idlis.
