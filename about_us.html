About TastyGrid

We’re a small project dedicated to preserving home recipes and making cooking joyful and repeatable. TastyGrid helps you save family recipes, share tips, and discover flavors across India.

Our mission: keep real food memories alive — by making recipes easy to read, adapt and share.
