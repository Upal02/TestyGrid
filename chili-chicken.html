Chili Chicken

A spicy, tangy Indo-Chinese favourite that’s sizzling on plates across India.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields4 servings • Prep: 20 min • Cook: 20 min

Ingredients

Marinade & Fry

  • 500 g boneless chicken, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp cornflour, 1 egg (optional), pinch salt & pepper
  • Oil for frying

Sauce

  • 3 tbsp oil, 4 garlic cloves chopped, 1″ ginger chopped
  • 1 onion cut into squares, 1 capsicum cut into squares
  • 2–3 tbsp chili sauce, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ cup water, 1 tbsp cornflour slurry

Method

Marinate & fry

Marinate & fry: Marinate chicken for 15–30 minutes. Fry until crisp and golden. Drain and set aside.

Make sauce

Make sauce: Sauté garlic, ginger, onion whites and capsicum. Add chili sauce, soy, vinegar and sugar; add water and thicken with slurry.

Combine

Combine: Toss fried chicken in sauce 1–2 minutes until coated and hot. Garnish with spring onion greens.

Watch this recipe

Tips

To reduce oil: bake chicken at 220°C for 12–15 minutes and finish in sauce for a lighter option.
