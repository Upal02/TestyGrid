Samosa

Crispy deep-fried triangular pastry filled with spiced potatoes (and peas) — a beloved snack worldwide.

⭐ Rating4.6–4.8 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields12–14 samosas • Prep: 40–50 min • Cook: 15–20 min

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, 4 tbsp ghee (or oil), salt, water

Filling

  • 4 large potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • ½ cup green peas (optional), 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1–2 green chilies, ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder), salt to taste, fresh coriander

Method

Make dough

Make dough: Mix flour, salt and ghee; rub until crumbly. Add water and knead to a firm dough. Rest 30 minutes.

Prepare filling

Prepare filling: Heat oil, add cumin & mustard seeds till crackling. Add ginger, chilies and peas; add mashed potatoes, turmeric, garam masala, amchur and salt. Mix well and cool. Stir in coriander.

Shape & fry

Shape cones: Divide dough into 10–12 balls. Roll into oval discs, cut in half, form a cone from each half, fill with potato mixture and seal edges.

Fry: Heat oil to medium and deep-fry samosas in batches until golden brown and crisp. Drain on paper towels.

Watch this recipe

Tips

Don’t overfill cones and seal edges tightly. Maintain medium oil temperature — too hot will brown outside and leave inside undercooked. Leftover samosas can be reheated in the oven for crispness. Serve hot with tamarind & mint chutneys.
