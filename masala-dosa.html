Masala Dosa

Crispy dosa with spicy potato masala — Udupi classic breakfast.

⭐ Rating4.8 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields~8 dosas
TimePrep 6–12 hrs (ferment) • Cook 20–30 min

Ingredients

Batter

  • 2 cups parboiled rice (idli rice) + 1 cup regular rice
  • ¾ cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1 tsp fenugreek seeds, water for soaking/grinding, salt
  • Ferment overnight (6–10 hrs)

Potato Masala

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp urad dal
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced; 1–2 green chilies; 8–10 curry leaves
  • ½ tsp turmeric, salt, fresh coriander

Method

Batter prep

Soak: Soak rice and urad dal+fenugreek separately 4–6 hours.

Grind & ferment: Grind urad to a smooth, fluffy batter and rice to slightly grainy batter. Mix, add salt and ferment overnight until slightly sour and airy.

Make potato masala

Temper: Heat oil, add mustard seeds and urad dal; when they sputter add curry leaves, chilies and onions. Sauté till soft.

Combine: Add turmeric and mashed potatoes, salt; mix and finish with chopped coriander.

Make dosa

Cook: Heat a heavy tawa, pour a ladle of batter and spread thin in concentric circles. Drizzle oil and cook until crisp and golden. Spoon potato masala on one half and fold.

Serve: Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

Tips

Batter should be pourable but not watery. Use a well-seasoned cast-iron or nonstick tawa for best results.

Notes & Serving

Adjust batter consistency for spreadability; thinner dosas will be crispier. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar.
