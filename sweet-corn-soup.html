Sweet Corn Soup (Vegetarian)

A silky, comforting Indo-Chinese soup featuring sweet corn, tender carrot and a glossy, lightly thickened broth. Vegetarians can skip the egg for a fully plant-based version.

⭐ Rating4.3–4.5 ★
DifficultyEasy
Yields4 servings • Prep 5–10 min • Cook 10–12 min

Ingredients

Soup base

  • 4 cups vegetable stock (hot & flavorful)
  • 1 cup sweet corn kernels (fresh or canned, drained)
  • 1 small carrot, finely diced (~½ cup)
  • Optional: 2–3 tbsp sweet corn purée (blend a few kernels with stock for body)

Thickener & seasoning

  • 2–3 tbsp cornflour mixed with 3–4 tbsp water (slurry)
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • Salt to taste, ¼–½ tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional finishing aroma)
  • Egg — 1, lightly beaten (optional — omit for vegetarian/vegan)
  • Spring onion greens — thinly sliced for garnish

Method

1. Simmer vegetables

Simmer: Bring vegetable stock to a gentle boil. Add diced carrot and corn kernels; simmer 5–7 minutes until the carrot is tender but not mushy. If using corn purée, stir it in now for extra body.

2. Season

Season: Add soy sauce and white pepper. Taste and adjust salt carefully — soy adds saltiness.

3. Thicken

Thicken: Re-mix the cornflour slurry (it settles). With the soup at a gentle simmer, pour the slurry slowly while stirring continuously until the broth becomes glossy and lightly coats a spoon. Cook 1–2 minutes.

4. Egg ribbons (optional)

Egg (optional): Reduce heat. Slowly drizzle beaten egg in a thin stream while stirring in one direction to form delicate egg ribbons. Omit for strict vegetarian/vegan versions.

5. Finish & serve

Finish: Turn off heat, add a tiny drizzle of toasted sesame oil if using, garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot. Pairs well with crispy fried noodles or dim sum.

Tips & Notes

• Add slurry slowly — you can always thicken more but it's hard to thin a pastey soup.
• For creamier texture, blend 2–3 tbsp corn kernels with stock and add back.
• Store in fridge up to 2 days; reheat gently and add a splash of stock if too thick.
• Make vegan: omit egg and use sesame oil or extra corn purée for silkiness.
