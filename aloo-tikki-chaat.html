Aloo Tikki Chaat

Crisp potato patties topped with chickpeas, cool yogurt, chutneys and sev — a balance of textures and flavors.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields4 servings • Prep: 20 min • Cook: 15–20 min

Ingredients

Tikki

  • 4 large potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 1 tsp chaat masala, 1 tsp roasted cumin powder, salt & chili powder to taste
  • ¼ cup breadcrumbs (for binding)
  • Oil for shallow frying

To serve

  • Boiled chickpeas, yogurt, tamarind & mint chutneys, sev, chopped onions & coriander

Method

Make patties

Make patties: Mix mashed potatoes with spices and form into patties; lightly coat with breadcrumbs.

Shallow fry

Shallow fry: Fry on medium heat until crisp and golden.

Assemble chaat

Assemble: Place tikki on plate, top with boiled chickpeas, spooned yogurt, both chutneys, sev, sprinkling of chaat masala and coriander.

Watch this recipe

Tips

Balance the chutneys — sweet tamarind and spicy mint — to get the classic chaat profile. Make tikki a bit thicker if you want a sturdy base to hold the toppings. Best served immediately for maximum crunch.
