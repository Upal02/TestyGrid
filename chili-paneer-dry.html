Chili Paneer (Dry)

Paneer friability and texture make it ideal for Indo-Chinese tosses.

⭐ Rating4.5 ★
DifficultyMedium
Yields3–4 servings • Prep: 15 min • Cook: 12–15 min

Ingredients

Paneer & Coating

  • 400 g paneer, cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 3 tbsp cornflour + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour for coating
  • Salt & pepper to taste, oil for shallow frying

Sauce & Veg

  • 2 tbsp oil (for sauce), 3 garlic cloves minced, 1″ ginger minced
  • 1 small onion, cut into square pieces; 1 small capsicum, cut into squares
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp chili sauce, 1 tsp vinegar, 1 tsp sugar
  • 2–3 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp water + 1 tsp cornflour (slurry)

Method

Coat paneer

Coat paneer: Toss paneer cubes in cornflour + maida + salt + pepper.

Fry paneer

Fry paneer: Shallow-fry in oil until edges turn golden. Drain and set aside.

Make sauce

Make sauce: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok; sauté garlic & ginger. Add onion whites and capsicum; toss to retain crunch. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar and sugar. Add slurry to make sauce glossy.

Combine

Combine: Gently toss fried paneer into the sauce so cubes remain intact. Heat 1–2 minutes, finish with green spring onion and sesame oil (optional).

Serve

Serve: As starter or with fried rice/noodles.

Watch this recipe

Tips

Don’t overcrowd the pan while frying paneer; fry in batches for crisp exterior. For a lighter version skip frying and toss paneer directly (texture will differ).
